Balancer Update Accounts Setting & Roles In Open Periods
========================================================

Update Account Settings and Roles
---------------------------------

Changes to account settings and roles are automatically applied to:

*   Accounts in open periods without uploaded balances
*   Accounts in unopened periods

To update account settings and roles for accounts with balances, follow the steps below:  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/91a510c30847397d285c6e39bc989b60.jpg)

1.  Navigate to **Jobs>>Other jobs>>Update settings and roles**. 

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/8fca60d88fe1f51a3387a1a8e2f331b0.jpg)

2.  **Select period** – Select the correct period.
3.  **Legal entities** – Check **Update account settings** and/or **Update account roles** to apply the update universally or for individual legal entities.
4.  **Update** – Click to execute the update job. A job log line appears on the **Jobs** page.

 **WARNING**: If sub-accounts are added or removed from a group account, the updated configuration will not be reflected during periods with imported balances. The specific group accounts and sub-accounts must be removed for the designated period in **Administration>>Delete>>Accounts in period**. See [Related\_Articles](#related-articles) for more information.
