Matcher Important Terms and Concepts
====================================

Key Terms & Definitions
-----------------------

 

**Term**

**Definition**

Account

A collection of transaction items or balances used in matching. Examples include bank, ledger, or processor accounts.

Balancer

An Adra application that provides a more streamlined reconciliation process, increased visibility, and knowledge sharing.

Exceptions

Transaction items that were not matched during the automatic matching process and need to be matched manually.

Extra Fields

Informational fields that can be added to transactions and exported to Balancer.

Fiscal Year

A 12-month period used for accounting and financial statements.

Legal Entity

An individual, company, or organization with legal rights and obligations.  In Matcher, legal entities might be used to define companies or store locations.

Matching Rule

Rules that automatically match transactions within a reconciliation group according to criteria (date, amount deviation, and desired additional fields) defined by administrators.

Period

A specified length of time (month, quarter, or year) used for measuring fiscal, or financial, results.

Reconciliation

The process through which transactions or records are matched together to ensure the general ledger is accurate.

Reconciliation Groups

A collection of accounts where transactions are to be matched against each other.

Shared Ruleset

A collection of matching rules that can be used by multiple reconciliation groups.

Transaction

An item that appears on a statement that can be matched against other items.
