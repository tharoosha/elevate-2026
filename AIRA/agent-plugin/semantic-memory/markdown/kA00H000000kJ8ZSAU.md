On the **General Settings page**, administrators can manage settings at the engagement level and the legal entity level.  
Navigate to **Administration>>General Settings.**   
  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c0a1fa7a63f3fc118eab38853a4022bb.jpg)  
  
The settings are sorted into three categories:

*   **Currency Settings** – Shows the engagement's currency. To change the engagement's currency, navigate to Setup>>Engagement. To learn more, see [Setup Engagement Settings](#article:kA00H000000oOTGSA2@Success-Center).
*   **Certification Settings** – Enable or disable settings related to certifying accounts.
*   **Other Settings** – Manage all other engagement settings.

  
Most of the settings have a switch that indicates whether the setting is enabled or disabled  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/521a4174415a9b570591dba9e449b89c.jpg)

*   **Blue** – The setting is enabled.
*   **Grey** – The setting is disabled.

Once the desired changes have been made in each category, click **Save** to apply the updates.  
 

Currency Settings
-----------------

### Engagement Currency  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/e6df130847a936cbeac087531a84827a.jpg)

*   From the drop-down, select an engagement currency.
*   This currency is used as the base currency for currency calculations and facilitates easy comparison between legal entities on the **Dashboards** and the **Home** page.

Certification Settings
----------------------

### Enforce Segregation of Duties

*   **Enabled** – Once a user has completed a certification step for an account, they are restricted from performing any other certification steps for the same account.
    *   **Example** – The user who prepared the account cannot approve or review it.

### Require Status on All Transactions

*   **Enabled** – All transactions added to an account must be assigned a **Status** before the account can be prepared. If the status is missing, preparing the account results in an error message.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c4fb44df0b19eb036a341a8e012d24b4.jpg)  
 

### Enable Bulk Approve Action

*   **Enabled** \- Approvers can approve accounts in bulk on the **Accounts and status** 

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/2f23244e4516dcd3c12cc3dff24e6d8b.jpg)  
To use the functionality, navigate to **Account and status** page, select accounts to approve, click **Bulk action** on the bottom left.

Learn more about bulk approval of accounts [Balancer Accounts and Status Page Overview](#article:kA00H000000kJ8eSAE@Success-Center).

### Enable Certification Thresholds

The certification threshold serves as a safety measure when accounts are prepared manually or through a System certify job. If unidentified balances exceed the thresholds, the user will be alerted or prevented from preparing the account. When using the System Certify feature, the account will not be prepared. See [Related Articles](#related-articles) to learn more.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/17f3df4df6f97d33aaa63c210af1ca9a.jpg)

1.  **Enabled certification thresholds** – The account will not be certified when the legal entity's threshold is exceeded. 
2.  **Don't prevent certification** – If checked, the Preparer receives a notification when the certification threshold is breached, but the account can still be prepared.
3.  **Set Thresholds** – Click to open the threshold pop-up.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/36e68efcd85420c6851f74e6f02deaef.jpg)  
 

1.  1.  **Amount** – Enter threshold amount. The amount includes both positive and negative    changes in the balance.
    2.  **And/Or** – Select **And** to notify or prevent preparer when both the amount and percentage thresholds are exceeded.  
        Select Or to notify or prevent preparers when one of the thresholds is exceeded.
    3.  **Percent** – Enter the max percentage at which the balance is permitted to change.
    4.  **Save thresholds** – Click to apply the thresholds.  
         

### Enable Variance Analysis

This functionality allows additional control and visibility of account balance variance between periods. When accounts are prepared, an explanation of the variance is required if the thresholds are exceeded.

*   **Enabled** – The **Set threshold** button is displayed. Click to open the **Set variance threshold** pop-up. 

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/624d2337a36189c8bd9e5132c73d66d9.jpg)

*   Select the type of comparison in the drop-down.
*   Enter the threshold amount and percent. The account will be flagged if both thresholds are exceeded.

Other Settings
--------------

### Allow Cross Legal Entity Groups to Be Created

*   **Enabled** \- Group accounts with sub accounts from different legal entities can be created. 
*   This setting cannot be disabled if a cross entity group has been created.

### Show Comments from Previous Periods

*   **Enabled** \- Comments that are added to an account’s page follow the account in succeeding periods. 

### Prevent Non-finalized Period from Being Closed

*   **Enabled** \- BA and LBA are unable to close periods on the Periods page if there are any accounts that have not been finalized.  

### Show Only Accounts for Which the User Has an Account Role

*   **Enabled** – Standard users can only view accounts associated with assigned roles on the Accounts and status page and the Home page.
*   **Disabled** – Standard users can view all accounts in assigned legal entities.

### Enable Dashboard for All Users

*   **Enabled** – Standard users have access to **Dashboards**.

### Prevent Auditors from Being Assigned Account Roles

*   **Enabled** – BAs and LBAs cannot assign account roles to an auditor. Auditors will not be listed under **Frequency and roles** in **Account settings**.

### Show Only Finalized Accounts to Auditors

*   **Enabled** – Auditors cannot view accounts that are not finalized, safeguarding sensitive information.

### Prevent Copying or Transferring of Roles for Legal Entities Where the User Doesn't Have Access

*   **Enabled** \- Standard users cannot be granted access to a legal entity accidentally when administrators use the copy and transfer roles functionality.

### Prevent Periods from Being Started Unintentionally

*   **Enabled** – The checkbox Open all unopened legal entities on the **Review** page for a balance import defaults to unselected.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/8988a0b9e20c6f99ba181b1e9955b8a9.jpg)

**WARNING**: For auto-imported balance files, the threshold in the **Job automation** settings overrides **Prevent periods from being started unintentionally**. To ensure periods are not started accidentally when **Job automation** is used:

1.  Navigate to **Administration>>Job automation>>Balance import**.
2.  Click **Use custom thresholds** if not already enabled.
3.  Click **New balance file is uploaded for a period that is not created or started**.

### Use Custom Import Definition for Balance or Transaction Import

Utilize XML to enhance flexibility for the balance and/or the transaction import. Typically, a consultant from Trintech handles the setup of this functionality.

*   Import files with different column orders than the standard import.
*   Import files with other delimiters than the standard import (Tab, XLS, XLSX) and position delimited files.
*   Combine columns into one column and test on rows to include or exclude rows.
