Matcher User Page
=================

In addition to have access to Matcher in Setup, Standard Users and Limited Access Users must be given access to legal entities and reconciliation groups to enable them to perform required tasks. 

*   To learn how to create users and grant access to Matcher, see [Setup Create New Users In Adra](#article:kA00H000000kJ5LSAU@Success-Center).
*   To understand the user role access privileges, see [Matcher Understanding User Roles and Access.](#article:kA0Ua0000006elxKAA@Success-Center)

On the **Users** page in Matcher, administrators can view a list of users who have access to Matcher, including their access levels and whether they are inactive.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/3349f7cdec9aad71dfebf893d09c7878.jpg)

*   Click **Create User** to navigate to the **Setup** page to add new users.
*   Toggle **Show inactive users** to view users who are inactive in **Setup** but still have Matcher assigned on the **Access** tab.

To grant **Standard Users** and **Limited Access Users** permission to work with the organization’s legal entities and reconciliation groups, go to the **Users** page, click on the user (they won’t have a badge in the **Roles** column), and assign the appropriate **Access** and/or **Local Business** privileges.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/91ac2ad9e962eac1faea56699d4df214.jpg)   

1.  **Grant access to all**
    *   **Grant access to all legal entities** \- Check to give access to all current and future legal entities.
    *   **Grant local business admin privilege access to all legal entitie**s - Check to give Business Admin access to all current and future legal entities. This option is unavailable unless **Grant access to all legal entities** is checked.
2.  **Filters -** Click a funnel icon to filter the legal entities. When the User Access box or the Local business admin box is checked, only the visible legal entities are selected.
3.  **Legal entities** - Check individual legal entities to limit access to only those selected. 
4.  **Reconciliation groups** - Expand a legal entity to view this option. Check individual legal entities to limit access to only those selected.
    *   Toggle **Grant access to all reconciliation groups on this legal entity** to ensure the user has access to both current and future reconciliation groups created within the selected legal entity.
