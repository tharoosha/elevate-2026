Balancer Periods Page Overview
==============================

The period page provides an overview of unopened, opened, closed, and archived periods. It also allows administrators to import balances to a period to open it. Click a period in any state to open its **Manage period** page.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/30f2c92123d4005f33acf381bd83df50.jpg)

1.  **Import new period** - Click to open the **Import account balances** page.
2.  **Tabs** \- Select a tab to view periods with the relevant status.
3.  **Periods** \- Click a period to view the **Manage period** page.
    *   System certification can be reverted here for open periods.

Open Periods
------------

Navigate to **Periods>>Open Periods**  
This tab displays all periods that have been opened and remain active.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f78d191ed0a7d654ec5e16dbc7f62123.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity and period** – Select legal entity and period from the drop-downs to view data accordingly.
2.  **Close selected** –  Click to close selected periods.
3.  **Select period** – Check the periods that are to be closed.

Click a legal entity name to view period details.

### Close a period

To close a period, check one or more legal entities on the**Open periods** tab, then click **Close periods**. A pop-up appears.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/80e97ea4f500760383447a21eabe34ed.jpg)

*   **Close only periods with finalized accounts** – Check to ensure no periods are closed when the status of any account is not finalized. This option is automatically activated when the Prevent Non-Finalized Period from being closed setting is enabled in the General settings.
*   **Close period(s)**\-Click to close the period.

Closed Periods
--------------

When a period is finalized, it can be closed. The period will then be listed under the **Closed periods** tab. The period will still be available to view on the **Accounts and status** page, but no further changes can be made to the accounts.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/d993190b2240a9dbad9f49a66e6674a6.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity and period** – Select legal entity and period from the drop-downs to view data accordingly.
2.  **Reopen** **selected** – Click to reopen selected periods.
3.  **Archive Selected** – Click to archive selected periods.
4.  **Select period** – Check the periods that are to be opened. Click a legal entity name to view period details.
5.  **Notify** **assigned** **users** – Check to send a notification email to users that actions are required on assigned accounts.

Unopened Periods
----------------

The Periods listed under **Unopened periods** have had balances uploaded to them, but the period was not opened during the review of the import.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f002199fbd85628fded02ca6d62257cf.jpg)

1.  **Import new period**– Click to open the **Import account balances** page.
2.  **Legal entities** – Select a legal entity from the drop-down to sort the periods.
3.  **Open selected** – Click to open selected periods.
4.  **Select period** – Check the periods that are to be opened.
    *   Click a legal entity name to view period details.
5.  **Notify assigned users** – Check to send a notification email to users that actions are required on assigned accounts.

Click **Ok** in the pop-up.  
 

 **HELPFUL HINT**: When a period is categorized as unopened, it can be viewed but not modified on the account and status page. Opening the period is necessary to enable the preparation of accounts

### Import New Period

Periods can be opened in two locations;  
**Jobs>>New job>>Import balances across multiple legal entities**.  
or  
**Periods>>Import new period**  
When opening a period from the Periods page, an import page loads with options to upload balances exclusively to unopened periods.  
 ![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/b1c2cc70a97ac397417fe5f85965d103.png)

1.  **Template** – Click the blue link to download a balance file template.
2.  **Period** – Select what period to open in the drop-down.
3.  **Upload file** – Drag and drop balance files or click **Upload file** to locate them locally on the computer.
4.  **Import** – Click to import the file.

The **Jobs** page loads.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/bccb6535b6a8e61f8cd9a2df1142ba0e.png)

5.  Click **Review and approve** in the **Action required** field.

  
 

### Review the import

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/da6ef7344ddcebcba7812ca7c64a82e7.jpg)

1.  **View import messages** – Click to view potential errors with the import file.
2.  **Import settings** – To automatically import balances to all legal entities in the file, check **Import to all legal entities**. To permit periods for all legal entities in the file to be opened by the import, check **Open all unopened legal entities**.
3.  **Legal entities** – To import only some legal entities, uncheck the import box for one or more legal entities.
4.  **Review** – Click to open a review pop-up, to verify the updated balances.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/e35304c9d42db79fc624503d6818542d.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity** – sort by legal entity if necessary.
2.  **Select** – Check **Select all** or individual accounts.
3.  **OK** – Click to verify selected balance changes.

5.  **Complete job** - Click to import updated balances.

Archived Periods
----------------

Archiving periods removes them from the period drop-down in the left-hand menu. It also enables the period to be deleted.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/195a505060172ff75ff3a2a0506367c1.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity and period** – Select legal entity and period from the drop-downs to view data accordingly.
2.  **Move to closed** – Click to move selected periods back to closed.
3.  **Select period** – Check the periods that are to be moved to **Closed periods**.

Click a legal entity name to view period details.   

 **HELPFUL HINT**: If **Prevent Non-Finalized Period from being Closed** is enabled in General settings under the Administration drop-down, only periods where all accounts are finalized can be closed.

Manage a Period
---------------

To manage a legal entity’s period, navigate to **Periods**, and click the tab the period is sorted under. Click the legal entity in question to open the **Manage period page**.  
The page allows administrators to:

*   Revert system certifications.
*   View the activity log for the period.
*   View balances according to account classes.
*    View and be directed to accounts by status.
*   Open, re-open, or close the period, depending on the period’s current status.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/7d5a1bb8041407e4f10052d91019a5cb.jpg)

1.  **Revert system certification accounts** – Revert all system certified accounts to the Not prepared status.
2.  **Activity log** – Click to see the user's activity, including the specific activity, date, and time.
3.  **Total balance sheet** – Period balances calculated from account classes.

On the **Manage** period page, the second section shows the count of accounts in different statuses and finalized states. Click a specific number to be redirected to the Accounts and Status page, where those particular accounts are displayed.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/52077ab1003e76c0182bab0d15def10d.jpg)

The action button in the bottom right of the page allows administrators to move the period to Open, Closed, or Archived, depending on what status the period currently has.
