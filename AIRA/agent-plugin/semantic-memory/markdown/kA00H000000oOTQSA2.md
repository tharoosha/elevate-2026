Setup Adra in Japanese
======================

Adra in Japanese
----------------

  
The Adra products now support Japanese language and date/time/number format.  
  
Each individual user must select their preferred language in their own profile. To manage your profile's language, follow these steps: 

1.  **Setup >>** **Profile**
2.  Under **Date Time Format**, click the option for Japanese. 
3.  Under **Language**, click the option for Japanese. 
4.  Click **Save**.
