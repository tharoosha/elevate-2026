Balancer Update Group Accounts in Active Periods
================================================

When adding or removing sub-accounts from a group account, these changes automatically apply to periods that have not yet started or to accounts where no balances have been imported. If the changes to the group account need to take effect for an account with a balance in an ongoing period, the account must first be deleted for that period. Follow the steps below:  
 

1.  Make the necessary changes to the group account.
    
2.  Navigate to **Administration>>Delete>>Accounts in Period**.
    
3.  Check the sub-account(s) that have been included or excluded from the group,  and click **Delete**.![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/d429b404b86f98059475d32376f90d9a.jpg)
    
4.  After implementing the changes, re-upload the balance for the deleted account.
    

   

 **HELPFUL HINT**: The account group's status must either have the status rejected state or not prepared to appear on the **Delete** page. Accounts can be rejected or reverted in two ways.

1.  Upload a new, fictional balance to the account that needs to be deleted. The account will be reverted as not prepared
2.  Navigate to **Account and status**, open the group account and click **Reject account**.
