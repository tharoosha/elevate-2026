Setup Add BPO Organization Admins and Remove External Admins
============================================================

Remove External Administrator
-----------------------------

External admins, such as Adra employees, may be added to the organization during configuration or as a result of a support request.   
  
To remove an External administrator:

1\. Navigate to **Setup>>Organization**.  
2\. Click the **Organization administrator** tab.  
![Organization Administrator.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/8e19320baabb80b57b23ac67d777892a.jpg)

  
Add BPO Organization Admin
-----------------------------

BPO Admins can add colleagues from within the BPO Organization as administrators. Each organization must have at least one administrator.  
  
To add a BPO employee as a BPO Organization Admin:  
1\. Navigate to **Setup>>Organization**.  
2\. Click the **Organization administrator** tab.  
![Organization Administrator.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/0e60972dff789d0cb936a0eda5de9210.jpg)  
 

3\. Click the **Add administrator** field. A list of BPO employees with accounts under the BPO Organization displays.  
4\. Select the employee or employees to add as BPO admin(s).  
5\. Click **Add administrator**.  
![Add Administrator.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/40dbd3b842674490a497ebdd896d16fa.jpg)
