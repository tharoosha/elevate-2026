Balancer Open a Period
======================

In Balancer, periods can be opened in two ways:

1.  Automatically when balances are uploaded on the **Jobs** page
2.  On the **Periods** page if the period was selected not to open during the balance import.

To learn how to upload balances, see [Balancer Import Balances & Transactions](#article:kA0Ua0000000SU9KAM@Success-Center).

The opening balance period closes automatically after importing balances. To reimport, reopen the balance on the **Closed** tab of the **Periods** page. To learn more, see [Balancer Opening Balance](#article:kA06S0000019MDJSA2@Success-Center).

Upload Balances Without Openeing The Period
-------------------------------------------

To upload balances without opening the period, first upload the file as normal. 

On the Jobs page, do the following:

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/670f53b81d41c929f43b15f05a0f88fd.jpg)

Click **Review and approve** for the upload. 

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c946302e4c7623c9bf46ddcc9a7b9f5d.jpg)

Whether the **Open** check box is selected or not depends on the setting **Prevent periods from being started unintentionally** on the **General Settings** page.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/ac640d51c2edce973b383e2862603e2e.jpg)

*   If the setting is enabled (blue) the **Open** box will be unselected. The period must then be opened manually from the **Periods** page
*   If the setting is disabled (grey) the **Open** check box is automatically selected, and the period will open automatically.

Unselect or select the open box as needed. 

To open a period where the **Open** box was not selected, do the following:

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/356772dc27e7f7ad3b8003ce0aa17eac.jpg)

Navigate to **Periods>>Unopened Periods**.

Locate and select the correct period, and click Open Selected.

The accounts and balances will then be displayed on the **Accounts and status** page.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: While balances can be uploaded to a period without opening it, a period cannot be opened without uploading balances first.
