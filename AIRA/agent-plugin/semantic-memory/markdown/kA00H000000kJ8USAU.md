Setup Create & Manage Legal Entities
====================================

A legal entity is an individual, company, or organization with legal rights and obligations. 

*   In **Balancer**, legal entities are used to define and collect accounts for a company. They can also include relevant information, such as the operating currency, for the accounts within the company. All accounts must be connected to a legal entity.
*   In **Matcher**, legal entities are used to define and group reconciliation processes for a company. Within each legal entity, users create reconciliation groups that bring together the sources (such as bank statements or GL accounts) to be matched. All reconciliation groups must belong to a legal entity.

The **Legal entities** page is used to manage single or multiple companies. Administrators can access the list of existing legal entities and sort them according to their specific requirements.  
 

Create a New Legal Entity
-------------------------

To create a legal entity, do one of the following:

*   Navigate to **Administration>>Legal entities** in either Balancer or Matcher and click **Create new legal entity**. 
*   In Setup, navigate to **Financial Configuration>>Legal entities**, and click **Add legal entity**.  
    
*   Import multiple legal entities in Setup. To learn more, see [Setup Import Legal Entities on the Jobs Page](#article:kA0Ua0000002KqfKAE@Success-Center).

  
If you create a legal entity manually, the **Legal entity** pop-up opens as shown in the figure below. Complete all required fields (asterisk) and click **Save**.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/d5643b10ec150c09214de925a8e821d5.jpg)

*   **Name** – Enter the legal entity name.
*   **Code** – Enter a combination of letters and/or numbers used to identify the legal entity. When files are imported, Balancer uses this code to route transaction, balances, and settings to the specified legal entity.
    *   Examples: 001, A1, TRINTECH.
*   **Country** – Select the legal entity country. The Operating currency automatically populates based on this field.
*   **Operating currency** – Edit the default currency for the legal entity if applicable. 
*   **Additional information** – Enter the organization number and address. This information is useful for the audit process.
*   **Active** – The legal entity can be activated or deactivated as needed. While all data is preserved, new periods cannot be started for a deactivated legal entity.

 **BEST PRACTICE**: **Use Legal Entities to group accounts geographically and/or primary currency used.**

Edit Legal Entity
-----------------

Legal entities can be edited from **Setup**.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c4f5796c4b041a978ae449cab056b5f9.jpg)

1.  Navigate to **Setup>>****Administration>>Financial configurations>>Legal entities**
2.  Click a legal entity to edit it**.**

![Picture2.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c67961351aef35dbfe5eed7253f7a04a.jpg)

3.  Change or add any additional information.
4.  Click **Save** when finished.
