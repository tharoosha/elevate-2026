Balancer Automatically Generated Emails
=======================================

Balancer E-mails
----------------

E-mails are automatically generated by Balancer when jobs are completed through the Jobs page or Integration Hub.  
  
The figure below shows an example of a typical Balancer e-maill. The text and subject of automatically generated emails vary based on the job completed.  
 ![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/ee7df44202813adac8183752eaa16c51.jpg)

Balancer E-mails by Job
-----------------------

The text in the e-mail depends on what job has been done in Balancer, and whether it failed or was successful.  
The tables below explain the Balancer jobs that result in automatically generated e-mails.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/66492b9c68725106bca11dd451e48dd4.jpg)  
 

 **HELPFUL HINT**: There is no setting to prevent the creation of these e-mails. Create a filter in your inbox to send automatically generated emails to a separate folder.
