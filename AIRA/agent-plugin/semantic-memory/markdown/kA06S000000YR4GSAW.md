Balancer Carry Forward
======================

Carry Forward Requirements
--------------------------

 **WARNING**: Once you carry forward to a specific period, you cannot carry forward to the same period again.

  
Carry forward permits BA and LBA to move transactions and attachments from one period to another as long as criteria are met:

*   **Account status**– The account in the target period must have a status of **Not prepared** or **Rejected**.
*   **Job setting** – Navigate to **Account settings**, open an account and expand the **Job setting** section. Set **Carry forward** to one of two options:
    *   **10 - If carry forward rules are met**
    *   **20 - Always allow carry forward**

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/b79a998f28aec9df7ffbaf7077cf679d.jpg)  
  
 

The Carry Forward Job
---------------------

To carry items forward, navigate to **Jobs>>Other jobs>>Carry forward**.  
![rtaImage.jpg](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f6396f9bc7db4fb8b76085c668be676a.jpg) ​​​

1.  **Target period** – Select the period to transfer transactions and documentation to.
2.  **Reference period** – Select the period to transfer transactions and documentation from.
3.  **Legal entities** – Check **Select all** or individual legal entities.
4.  **Carry forward when** – Check rules to limit which accounts will carry forward.
5.  **Submit job** – Click to start **Carry forward** job.

When you run a carry forward job, the first option, The account is set to always carry forward, only impacts accounts with the **20 -** **Always carry forward** setting in the account settings. The two other job settings only impact accounts with the **10 - Allow carry forward** account setting.

The table below illustrates the relationship between an account’s carry forward setting and the carry forward job setting.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/6db943c5d4dd1c821b022e3d34b504f1.jpg)

Here is a deeper explanation of the latter two job settings:

*   **The balance in the target period is equal to the balance in the reference** **period** – Only when the balances in both periods are the same can documents and items be carried forward
*   *   Imagine a savings account where the balance stays the same unless someone adds or withdraws money. This setting assumes _no changes_ unless you manually update it.
*   **The registered items in the target period, if any, and registered items in the reference period add up to the balance in the target period** – You’re telling Balancer to start with last period’s items, add anything new I registered this period, and the total should match this period’s balance. If it does, carry forward the items and documents from the reference period.
    *   Let’s say you have a prepaid insurance account. Last period you had $1,000, and this month you add $200. This setting expects the total to be $1,200 unless some of the original amount was used up.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: If the accounts setting is set to **Always carry forward** it is exclusively influenced by the **The account is set to always carry forward** rule regardless of any other job settings.

Carry Forward Job Log
---------------------

After carrying forward accounts, click **Summary** in the **Action** **required** field on the Jobs page. 

#### ![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/be38ad7c3ea5b563ccee806777a6b1a2.jpg)

  
The figure below displays a **Carry forward** summary example. The summary displays what legal entities and rules were selected, and the amount of accounts carried forward.  
  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/4bfed183df0ccf0ced1f52b72ec63ea0.jpg)  
  
  
     

 **WARNING**: Once transactions and documentation are carried forward to a specific period, the period cannot be carried forward again. As an alternative solution, navigate to **Accounts and status**, open the account(s) in question, and click **Import items>>From prior period****.**
