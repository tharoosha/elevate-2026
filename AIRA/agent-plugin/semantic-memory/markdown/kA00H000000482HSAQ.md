Matcher Home
============

The Matcher **Home** page provides summary information, as well as a transaction to investigate panel.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/9ae274336e090247ffcbca9a3050762c.jpg)

1.  **Reconciliation Group Status** \- Click each button to load relevant information on the **Recon. Groups and Details** page.
    *   **Tile 1** - References the import status over the selected period of time.
    *   **Tile 2** - References the automatic matching status over the selected period of time.
    *   **Tile 3** - References the manual matching status over the selected period of time.
    *   **Tile 4** \- References the number of items sent to Balancer over the selected period of time.
    *   **Setting icon** - Click the gear icon in the top right corner to adjust the number of days in the view as well as toggle on and off the items they need to see.
2.  **Transactions to investigate** - View tasks that have been sent for investigation on the **Exceptions** page. To learn more, see [Matcher Exceptions Send for Investigation](#article:kA0Ua000000791lKAA@Success-Center).
    *   **Grid settings** 
        *   **Clear all filters** \- Click to remove any filters applied to each column.
        *   **Reset table** - Click to reset column visibility and order.
        *   **Columns** \- Click the toggles to enable or disable column visibility in the grid.
    *   **Filters**
        *   **Assigned to me toggle** \- Click to display investigations assigned to you only.
        *   **Created by me** - Click to display investigations that were created by you but might be assigned to someone else.
    *   **Columns**
        *   **Group investigations** - Drag one or more column headers into the **Drag and drop here to group** field to group items by that column’s categories. Group by multiple columns to create a waterfall effect. 
        *   **Column header** - Click to sort the items in ascending or descending order.
        *   **Funnel** \- Click any funnel to apply filters to the grid.
    *   **Action buttons** \- Hint at investigation status and allow users to view, manage, and complete investigations.
