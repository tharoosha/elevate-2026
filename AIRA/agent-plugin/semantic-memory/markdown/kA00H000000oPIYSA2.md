Matcher Three Way Matching Setup
================================

Three Way Matching
==================

Matcher allows for three way matching by transferring transactions to another reconciliation group. An example of this could be a ledger to credit card processor, which then needs to be matched to a bank statement.  
In this case, the first reconciliation group should be setup with ledger matching to the CC processor. Note, this may look different depending on your organization’s general settings. Once the first sources are selected, toggle on the **Transfer transactions to another reconciliation group** and click **+Add Account Link**. Note, both reconciliation groups will need to be setup prior to adding the link with the account information. Otherwise, the account being transferred to will not populate.   
  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/55a7b477f8b4139ab5ab3569043163be.jpg) 

After opening the **Add Account Link** popup, use the drop downs to select the required information.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/fd452d036a4a762747b48ef31c989631.jpg)

*   **Select source account (Current Reconciliation Group) -** The account that items should be transferred from the dropdown.
    
*   **Select target legal entity** – Select the correct legal entity for the account items are being transferred to.
    
*   **Select target account –** The account items are being transferred to for matching.
    
*   **Select link type –** Choose from one of the three options for transferring data then click **Add Link.**

The second reconciliation group setup should include the account items are being transferred to, plus the account they are being matched with. In the example, we are matching the CC Processor with the bank records in the second group. Once these settings are done, the administrator can return to the first reconciliation group to add the account links. Ensure **Save** is clicked for both reconciliation groups.
