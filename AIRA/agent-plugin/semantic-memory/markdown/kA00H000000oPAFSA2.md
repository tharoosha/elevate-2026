Adra Success Center eLearning
=============================

Benefits of eLearning
---------------------

Trintech’s eLearning courses provide hands-on, simulation-based product training segmented by role. eLearning helps customers learn how to perform activities that transfer directly to their day-to-day responsibilities. eLearning courses provide self-paced learning and are available 24/7 in the Success Center. Trintech offers CPE credit for eLearning for customers who seek this benefit.  
  
For customers who perform one-on-one peer training at their organizations, Trintech recommends eLearning as a precursor to this internal training. Customers can get new hires up to speed on the foundational knowledge of the product with eLearning so the company-specific processes can then be layered on top of the foundational product knowledge.  
  
eLearning is beneficial for customers to use as refresher training. Customers who have been using Trintech’s products for years may not be aware of the newest features and enhancements. eLearning helps customers learn about the latest and greatest updates to Trintech’s products.

  
Navigate to eLearning
------------------------

There are two ways to access eLearning courses:

*   From the **Learn** menu, expand **Self-Guided Courses** and then select the course you want.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/b8a28fb681dcf9ab2feaa6f77cf943d9.jpg)

*   From any Product Central page, click the **Self-Guided Courses** tab to access the eLearning courses for that specific product.
    

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/3a85b681c31109d576cd5767bf3f5d7a.jpg)

  
User and Administrator eLearning courses are identified by separate Learning Plans.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/d4552004a05424e677be9eaad8840774.jpg)

 **IMPORTANT**: All courses have open registration available to any  user or administrator.

*   eLearning courses are free. To begin an eLearning course, click to open the course and then click **Register**.
    
*   Each eLearning course contains an exam. The exam is optional. 
*   To earn CPE, complete the exam with a score of 70% or higher. 
    
*   Navigate to **My Activity** at any time to continue a course progress. **My Activity** is available from the menu in the top right of the Success Center.
    

**![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/6d15d4a54169e66299190ef853917ad8.jpg)**

As shown above, **My Activity** provides access to the following items:

1.  **Enrolled Courses**: Self-Guided Courses in progress. 
2.  **Completed Courses:** Self-Guided Courses completed.
3.  **All Courses:** Self-Guided Courses in progress and completed. 
4.  **Achievements**: Includes Learning Badges earned upon completion of eLearning courses and CPE Certificates that can be downloaded or printed.
5.  **Viewed Digital Content**: Documents, videos, and other resources accessed in the Success Center.
6.  **Followed Articles:** Knowledge Articles where you have clicked Follow.
7.  **Feed**: Includes a list of activity in Forums.
