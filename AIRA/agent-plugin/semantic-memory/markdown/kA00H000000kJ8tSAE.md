Balancer Create, Import & Manage Accounts
=========================================

There are three ways to add new accounts to Balancer:

*   Add one account at the time manually in **Administration>>Account settings>>New  account**
*   Add accounts through a balance import in **Jobs>>Import balances**
*   Add accounts in bulk through an account settings import in **Jobs>>Other jobs>>Import account settings** or **Administration>>Account settings>>Import.**

Learn how to create group accounts and cross entity group accounts: [Balancer Group Accounts and Cross Entity Group Accounts.](#article:kA00H0000015EUOSA2@Success-Center)

Add New Accounts Manually
-------------------------

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f5da6ab5bcf5612b244d99ae4a887aed.jpg)

1.  Navigate to **Administration>>Account settings>New account.**

The New account page loads:  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/535d1976da13c322a722872c28cbe966.jpg)

2.  Add necessary account details. Fields with an asterisk are mandatory.
    *   **Legal entity** – Select the legal entity associated with the account.
    *   **Account number** – Enter the account number from the ERP system.
    *   **Account name** – Enter the account name, for example from the ERP system.
    *   **Use the account as a group** – Make the account the primary account for a group, enabling the addition of sub accounts. 
    *   **Use account as a sub account to an existing group** – Add the account as a sub account to an existing group account.
    *   **Frequency and roles** – Select the reconciliation frequency and designate the responsible users.
    *   **Purpose and procedure** – Enter account management details.
    *   **Classification and extra fields** – Select account classifications. This feature aids in risk mitigation, sorting, and generating statistics.
    *   **Job settings** – Select job automation settings for the account, specifically for automating certification and transferring transactions and documentation between periods.
3.  Click **Save**.
    

  Learn more about account settings definitions: [Balancer Account Settings Explanations](#article:kA00H0000015ETuSAM@Success-Center)

Add New Accounts In Bulk
------------------------

This method is recommended to add multiple accounts simultaneously. 

1.  Navigate to **Jobs>>Other jobs>>Export account settings.**
2.  Add the new account(s) to the account settings file.
3.  Navigate to **Jobs>>Other jobs>>Import account settings.**
4.  Import the account settings file.
5.  **Approve and review** the account settings file.

Learn more here: [Balancer Export & Import Account Settings](#article:kA0Ua0000000TgLKAU@Success-Center)

Add New Accounts Through a Balance Import
-----------------------------------------

When new accounts are imported through a balance import job, the accounts can be configured in the approval step.

1.  **Balance file** – Ensure that the new accounts are included in the balance import file. 
2.  **Import** – Import the balance file. 

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f39f4123890a0379d1809d62c996fdca.jpg)

3.  **Review and approve** –  Click the review and approve link on the **Jobs** page.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/e04d4ff3e19dd2cb156126e0351d9ae6.jpg)

4.  **View -** Click to open the overview page.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/f92dc5578bc836bbdbe5eb440640734a.jpg)

5.  **Edit** \- Click the icon for each new account to add account settings. The **New accounts** page opens. Add applicable details and settings and click **Save**. Repeat for each new account.
6.  **Confirm selected** - Click to return to the **Review and approve** page for the balance import.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/6d6821dfc2651f3acf3d8bc2c1e69eb6.jpg)

7.  **Review all changes**  – Click to approve import.
8.  **Complete import** –  Click to complete import.
