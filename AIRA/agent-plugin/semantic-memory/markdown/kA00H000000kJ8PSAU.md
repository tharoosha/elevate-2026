Balancer Important Terms and Concepts
=====================================

Key Terms & Definitions

 

**Term**

**Definition**

Account

A collection of financial data within a legal entity that requires reconciliation to ensure that financial and bank records match.

Account Classes

The category an account represents on a balance sheet, such as Assets, Liabilities, or Equity. Account class is assigned in the Account Settings.

Account Status

The reconciliation stage of an account: not prepared, prepared, approved, reviewed, and finalized.

Classification

Optional information that can be selected when adding or editing an account, including account class, reconciliation type, key account, and risk rating. Classifications help administrators to organize accounts.

Closing Rate

Currency conversion rate between the engagement currency and the legal entity currencies and between the transaction currencies.

Extra Fields

Informational fields that can be added to accounts and transactions. The information contained in extra fields can be imported and used from the import files.

Fiscal Year

A 12-month period used for accounting and financial statements.

Frequency

The rate at which the steps in the reconciliation workflow - prepare, approve, and review - are completed for an account. Each step has an adjustable frequency; for example, an account can be set to be prepared and approved monthly but reviewed quarterly. 

Job Automation

Allows Balancer to auto-complete account reconciliations when importing balances and transactions. Administrators can set thresholds that require high-risk accounts to be reconciled manually.

Legal Entity

An individual, company, or organization with legal rights and obligations. In Balancer, legal entities are used to define and collect accounts for a company.

Legal Entity Code

Unique identifier used for reference when importing balances and settings to accounts. This code can be comprised of letters, numbers, and hyphens.

Period

A specified length of time (month, quarter, or year) used for measuring fiscal, or financial, results.

Reconciliation

Tasks performed to ensure the general ledger account balance is accurate. Reconciliations are prepared and then submitted for approval and review.

Reconciliation Type

A classification that identifies the account as either a Bank account or General Ledger account.

Transaction

A financial activity that is recorded in accounting records.
