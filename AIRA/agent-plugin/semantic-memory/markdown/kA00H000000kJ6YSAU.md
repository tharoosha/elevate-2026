Task Manager Home Page Overview
===============================

The **Home** page allows users to view tasks assigned to them or their team(s).

*   Preparers can view **Tasks in Progress** and **Upcoming Tasks**.
*   Approvers can view **Tasks to approve**.
*   Users who are both Preparers and approvers see all **Home** page sections.

Click any of the tasks on the Home page to view and manage it.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: To learn how to prepare and approve a task, see [Task Manager Prepare & Approve Tasks](#article:kA0Ua0000003Eb7KAE@Success-Center).

The figures below show examples of the **Home** page sections.

Tasks in Progress
-----------------

The **Tasks in Progress** section is visible to Preparers and displays tasks that have been started and are assigned to the current user.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/90e36deefac23847db53552d0456f010.jpg)

1.  **Balancer accounts assigned** - Click to view accounts assigned to the current user in Balancer that require action. Available only if Balancer is in use and the user has account roles for Balancer accounts.
2.  **Column headers**
    *   **Drag and drop here to group** \- Click and drag one or more headers to this field to group the list by the column content. 
    *   **Column headers** - Click a header to sort the task list in ascending or descending order. Move a header to another location to reorder the columns.
    *   **Funnel** \- Click to filter the list.
3.  **Toggles** \- Toggle both options to "on" to view a combined list. 
    *   **Overdue** \- Toggle to on to view overdue tasks.
    *   **Today** \- Toggle to on to view tasks due today.
4.  **Grid settings** - Click to manage visible task list columns, **Clear all filters**, or **Reset table**.
5.  **Task Status** - Hover over the task status icon with the mouse pointer to view the task status name.
6.  **Due Date** - A red due date indicates that the task is overdue. 
7.  **Actions** \- Click the star to prepare the task. 

Tasks to Approve
----------------

The **Tasks to approve** section is visible to Approvers and displays tasks that can be rejected or approved.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/9f6097ceabfc60b0eac886100d35fd70.jpg)

8.  **Tasks to approve Actions**
    *   **Check mark** \- Click to approve the task. 
    *   **X** \- Click to reject the task and send it back to the Preprarer.

Upcoming Tasks
--------------

The **Upcoming tasks** section is visible to Preparers and displays tasks that have not been started. These tasks are assigned either to a team the user is on or to individual users. 

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/56a5489e01a93241a7db819db3915e25.jpg)

9.  **Upcoming tasks actions**
    *   **Play** – Click the Play symbol to begin the task. This moves the task from **Upcoming Tasks** to **Tasks in Progress**. 
    *   **Star** – Click the Star button to prepare the task without clicking on it. An additional pop-up appears if **Enable certify** is enabled in **General Settings**. Note that this button is disabled for tasks identified as Internal Controls.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: Changes made to the order of the columns in the Upcoming Tasks section remain when users move between pages.
