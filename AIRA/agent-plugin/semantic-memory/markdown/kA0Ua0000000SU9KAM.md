Matcher Reassign User Roles
===========================

There are three roles in Matcher 

*   Business admin (configured in Setup)
*   Local Business Admins
*   Standard User

To change a standard users' access level in Matcher, navigate to Administration>>Users and click the standard user. Check the appropriate access boxes.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/710e8f92482a6024513d662970ddc0df.jpg)

*   Check the two uppermost boxes to grant access privileges and/or local business admin rights to all legal entities.
*   Check the applicable boxes for a legal entity to assign privileges for all reconciliation groups within said legal entity.
*   Click **expand** for a legal entity to grant access to specific reconciliation groups.

Click Save to retain changes.

Access level and privileges can only be assigned for individual users for Matcher.
