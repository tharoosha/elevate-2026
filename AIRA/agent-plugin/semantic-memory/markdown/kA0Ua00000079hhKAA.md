Matcher Limited Access User View
================================

Users given **Limited Access User** privileges in Setup can perform investigations on the assigned open transactions in Matcher. 

The image below displays the Limited Access User view.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/0a3e2e7abea33434dc5e5c220ced9aaf.jpg)

To navigate the view and perform an investigation, follow the steps below:

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/3025a7d35d38b6c6d2131b05787c16cd.jpg)

1.  **Settings icon**

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/09b6261ebb75d20be3d61526be1bc57d.jpg)

*   **Clear all filters** - Click to clear all filters applied to the grid.
*   **Reset table** \- Click to reset the order and visibility for the grid columns.
*   **Columns** \- Click toggles to hide or show columns.

2.  **Column headers**
    *   **Drag and drop here to group** - Click and drag one or more headers up to this field to group the listed items by that column's category.
    *   **Column header** \- Click to sort items in ascending or descencing order
    *   **Column order** - Click and drag columns sideways to reorder them.
    *   **Filter** \- Click a column funnel ![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/5d85eaef35cfdf26823f03751046e800.jpg) to filter content.
3.  **Transactions** \- Click a transaction to view details, add comments and finalize investigation.
4.  **Action buttons**

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/41a575efa5223f6d2a2845a994f1c73c.jpg)

Click a speech bubble or the transaction itself to open the **Investigation Details** pop-up.

![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/9001286bf42a18e005239bc14e725f68.jpg)

Enter a reply in the comment field. Click **Save** to keep the investigation in draft mode, so that it can still be edited, or click **Mark as Investigated** to finalize the investigation.
