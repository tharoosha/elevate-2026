Balancer Access for Standard Users & Auditors
=============================================

For standard users to be assigned preparer, approver, and reviewer account roles, access to the corresponding legal entities must be granted.  
Auditors also require read access to view and audit legal entities.  
Local Business Admin privileges can also be granted to permit standard users admin rights to specific legal entities.  
Refer to the table below for a user rights overview.  
![image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/d3b942658586a2478abe6a4664b2a9a2.jpg)  
 

Grant Legal Entity Privileges
-----------------------------

To grant access and rights, navigate to **Balancer>> Administration>>Users** and click the user.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/7187b05c978c98f30ba954e5f21ea1e1.jpg)

1.  **Access to all legal entities**
    *   **Grant access to all existing and future legal entities** – Check to apply read access to all legal entities.
    *   **Grant local business admin privileges to all existing and future legal entities** – Check to grant local business administrator rights to all legal entities. Click here to learn more.
2.  **Read access** – Check the boxes in the Read access column to enable account user roles in specified legal entities.
3.  **Local Business Admin** – Check the boxes in the Local BA column to provide BA privileges for specified legal entities. This option is not available for auditor users.

For Auditor users, the page only displays the **Read access** option.
