Setup Create Legal Entities
===========================

A legal entity is an individual, company, or organization with legal rights and obligations.  

Legal entities can be created manually or imported in bulk using the Integration Hub feature. Reach out to your customer representative to set this up. See [Related Articles](#related-articles) for more information.

To create a legal entity, navigate to **Setup>>Engagement>>Legal entities>>Add legal entity**. 

**![2024-03-22_12-51-47.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/adc4364301894d09b5727b102e00cc59.jpg)**

*   **Name** – Enter the legal entity name.
*   **Code** – Enter a combination of letters and/or numbers used to identify the legal entity. When files are imported, Balancer uses this code to route transactions, balances, and settings to the specified legal entity.
    *   Examples: 001, A1, TRINTECH.
*   **Country** – Select the legal entity country. The Operating currency automatically populates based on this field.
*   **Operating currency** – Edit the default currency for the legal entity if applicable. 
*   **Additional information** – Enter the organization number and address. This information is useful for the audit process.
*   **Active** – The legal entity can be activated or deactivated as needed. While all data is preserved, new periods cannot be started for a deactivated legal entity.

 **BEST PRACTICE**: **Use Legal Entities to group accounts geographically and/or the primary currency used.**
