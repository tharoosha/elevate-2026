Balancer Configure Customized Periods
=====================================

Companies commonly use a few non-traditional calendars to process reconciliations. **Balancer** can easily be customized to operate using these calendars. Below are the instructions for setting up these custom periods.

4-4-5 Calendar
--------------

The 4-4-5 calendar is a standard structure for industries such as retail and manufacturing. An accounting election of this calendar compared to a traditional accounting calendar is that the end date of a period is always on the same day of the week.   
  
The **Start date** and **End date** of the period can be custom-set as long as they fall within the start and end dates of the fiscal year.   
  
To make this customization: 

1.  Navigate to **Balancer>>Administration>>Fiscal years>>New fiscal year**.
2.  Select the **End date** for each of these months manually.
3.  If the end date of any period exceeds the fiscal year-end, **Balancer** will show an error, and the “End date” text will be red.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/e5bc391476d9bfbc3ae85c71c05baf41.jpg)

4.  When the **Fiscal year End date** is changed to allow for a period extending into the next calendar year (i.e., the period end date is on or before the year-end date), the system will enable the creation of the new period. 
5.  Enter all necessary information and click **Save**.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/32c50ff43144dba908d3b65c484b7294.jpg)

 **BEST PRACTICE**: The 4-4-5 calendar can be created using Quarter-end periods also, as long as the end date of the fiscal year has been extended appropriately.

   
 

Thirteen (13) Month Calendar
----------------------------

The thirteenth-month accounting period facilitates an accounting method that does not seek to make annual corrections or adjustments during any particular month or quarter because that would skew the financial reporting for that specific period. Instead, an artificial month is created as the thirteenth month. Bad debts, other write-offs, and income from unusual activities are booked in the thirteenth month and flow through for year-end accounting purposes.  
  
Like a traditional calendar, a 13-month accounting calendar is configured the same way as a conventional fiscal year calendar. With the exception of the 12th month, regular month-end periods have one less day (usually Dec 30th) and an extra (13th) month-end period consisting of only the last day of the fiscal year (usually Dec 31st).  The **Start date** and **End date** of periods can be customized within **Balancer** as long as they fall within the start and end date of the fiscal year.  
  
To make this customization: 

1.  Navigate to **Balancer>>****Administration>>****Fiscal year>>****New fiscal year**. 
2.  Like 4-4-5, the periods must be created within the fiscal year’s start date and end date. 
3.  Move the **End date** of **Period 12** forward one day, and the system will allow a 13th end period to be created for that last singular day.
4.  Click **Save**.
