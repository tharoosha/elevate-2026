Matcher Legal Entities
======================

What Is A Legal Entity?
=======================

A legal entity is by definition an individual, company or organization which has legal rights and obligations. In Matcher, legal entities are used to define companies. Legal entities are used in Matcher as a way to collect accounts that belong to a certain company. The legal entity can also contain information that is relevant for most, if not all, accounts in a company, for example, the operating currency. All accounts have to be connected to a legal entity.

Legal Entity Page Overview
--------------------------

The Legal entities screen is used to manage a company or companies. Administrators can view the list of existing legal entities, both active and inactive.

1.  From the **Matcher** home page, click the drop-down arrow to the right of **Administration**.
    
2.  Click **Legal Entities**.
    

This page only allows Administrators to view legal entities.

![User-added image](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/5ab060ff097165183ad827e56ae074ba.jpg)  
 

 **BEST PRACTICE**: The Legal Entity Code is marked in blue. This is the code needed in i.e. imports to identify which legal entity a transaction should be pushed to.

Clicking on a legal entity will redirect the user to the Setup area of the product. Here the user will be able to do changes to the legal entity name, code, currency or other information.  
  
  
**Creating a New Legal Entity**

1.  To create a new legal entity, the business administrator will need to navigate to [SETUP](https://setup.adra.com/engagements), click in on their engagement, **Legal entities** and then **Add legal entity**.

![add legal entity.jpg](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/33bfb1639681580e13baf9cf40a6e53d.jpg)

2.  Complete all required fields.
    

![Picture1.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/a321674da1ba023b302987c3c1264427.jpg)  
Required Fields Descriptions
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

*   **Name**\- the name of the legal entity
    
*   **Code**\- unique number or name that identifies the entity when importing balances, items, accounts, roles and extra fields 
    
    *   This is normally the same ID that identifies the entity in the organization's ERP. This must be added in order to import data into the system.
        
*   **Country**\- select the country where the entity is located.
    
    *   When selecting the country, the operating currency drop-down will automatically select the currency used in the selected country.
        
    *   For example: By selecting Sweden from the Country drop-down, the operating currency SEK is automatically selected.
        
*   **Operating currency**\- If the operating currency is different from the currency used in the country selected, select the correct currency for the entity.
    
*   **Active**\- selected when the entity is active and deselect if the entity is sold or terminated
    

Edit Legal Entity
-----------------

Legal entities can be edited from within **Matcher** or from **Setup**.

1.  From **Matcher** click on the **legal entity** in Administration->Legal entities.
    

![Picture2.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/317396497f899f537bf28b7c82a577cf.jpg)

2.  From the **Edit legal entity** screen, users can change or add any additional information to the **Legal entity**.
    

*   Only fields marked with an asterisk are required. Other fields can be filled in, but they are not required.
    

3.  Click **Save** when finished.
