Balancer Group Accounts and Cross Entity Group Accounts
=======================================================

Group accounts
--------------

Group accounts are recommended if a set of GL accounts are reconciled together. The trial balance is imported to each individual GL account, but the cumulative total will be the balance of the group account. The group account must be created before balances can be uploaded.  
To designate an account as the main account in a group, navigate to **Administrations>>Account settings** and click an account.  
 ![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/7c0aa5c8ec02132e696f438fd6477510.jpg)

1.  **Use the account as a group** – Enable to make the account the main account in a group.
2.  **Add sub accounts** – Click to open a pop-up.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/653a25c8ba269f76074d03ab83d6c50e.jpg)

3.  **Search field** – Enter the full or partial account number or name in the search field. 
4.  **Accounts** – Check a box to included the account as a sub account.
5.  **Add sub accounts** – Click to save group account changes.

 **WARNING**: Group accounts do not accept direct balance imports. Any balance data sent to a group account, whether via a manual import job or an automated integration, is discarded. The group account balance is calculated from its sub-accounts only. If your import jobs target an account that has been set as a group account, no balances will be imported.

Sub accounts
------------

Enable **Use account as a sub account to an existing group** to add the account as a sub account in an existing group, then select the group account in the drop-down.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/38a9b5a98ed105824e9257f6be2a3d71.jpg)

Converting an existing account to a group account
-------------------------------------------------

You can change an existing standard account to a group account or a sub account in **Administration >> Account settings** at any time. The change applies automatically to periods that have not yet started and to periods where no balances have been imported for that account.

The change does not apply to open periods where balances have already been imported. To apply the group structure to an open period with existing balance data, the account must first be deleted from that period. The account status must be either Rejected or Not prepared to appear on the Delete page.

**WARNING: If you need a converted account to take effect in an open period that already has balance data, you must delete the account from that period before the group structure will apply. Until you do, the account continues to behave as a standard account in that period.** 

Cross Entity Group Accounts
---------------------------

Cross entity group accounts allow accounts from different legal entities to be grouped and reconciled together. To use this feature, a **Business Admin (BA)** must activate it in **Administration >> General settings**. The main account must have **Cross Entity** as its legal entity. Sub-accounts must be created in a standard legal entity before being added to the cross entity group account.

Only a Business Admin (BA) can create or edit a cross entity group account. Local Business Admins (LBAs) can view and manage individual sub-accounts within their assigned legal entities, but cannot create or modify the group structure itself.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: If updated balances are imported to a cross entity group account, navigate to **Jobs >> Other jobs >> Update cross entity group balances** and run the job to refresh the balances. This job refreshes the calculated group total. Balance data must still be imported to the individual sub-accounts, not the main cross entity group account directly. 

 **WARNING**: All currency pairs needed for balance conversion must be configured before importing balances to cross entity sub-accounts. If a required currency pair is missing, no balance will be added to the cross entity group account and no error will be raised. Confirm currency pairs are in place in **Administration >> Closing rates** before running balance imports. 

To create the cross entity group, navigate to **Administration>>Account settings**, and follow the steps below.   
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/88e720dfca1c961aefecb2d17c698d02.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity** – Select Cross Entity.
2.  **New cross entity group** – Click to open the New Account page.

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/96002129d0507e01d11ddd87fc5cfee5.jpg)

3.  **Legal entity** – **Cross Entity** is automatically selected and cannot be changed.
4.  **Account number and Account name** – Enter the appropriate number and name for the cross entity group.
5.  **Add sub accounts** – Click to add accounts from different legal entities.
6.  **Remaining details** – Follow the standard procedure for creating a new account to add the remaining details.

 **WARNING**: When adding sub-accounts, verify each account is classified as a cross entity sub-account, not a regular sub-account. Incorrect classification creates separate group accounts within each individual legal entity instead of a single cross entity group. If cross entity groups are not appearing as expected on the Accounts and Status page, check sub-account classification in **Administration >> Account settings** for each sub-account. 

The **branching** icon on an account on any of Balancers pages indicates that the account is a main account or sub account:

![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/41ba3941c8903313857786458dadb3f4.jpg)  
 

 **HELPFUL HINT**: For group accounts and cross entity group accounts, the main account and sub account can be imported via the Jobs page, but the configuration of the groups must be done in Account settings. For cross entity group accounts, the legal entity code in the imported file must be SYSTEM.

 **WARNING**: When adding or removing sub-accounts from a group account, changes automatically apply to periods that have not yet started or to accounts where no balances have been imported. If the changes need to take effect for an account with a balance in an ongoing period, the account must first be deleted for that period. The account group status must be either Rejected or Not prepared to appear on the Delete page.
