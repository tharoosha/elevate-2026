Balancer Delete Accounts and Periods
====================================

The Delete page consists of three tabs:

*   **Accounts** – Delete accounts entirely from the system.
*   **Accounts in Period** – Delete accounts within a particular period.
*   **Periods** – Delete imported data (balances, transactions and attachments) from a period.

Click the **Delete activity logs** link in the bottom left corner of any page to view the delete history.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/39b8051c1978063ddb2329a8f074592a.jpg)  
    

 **WARNING**: Ensure that the correct period is selected from the drop-down on the left-side menu to avoid deletion from the wrong period. Deleting information can not be undone.

Delete Account From Chart of Accounts
-------------------------------------

Accounts can only be deleted if they are not used in any period. An account is removed entirely from Balancer when it is deleted from the **Accounts tab**.  
To delete an account, navigate to **Administration>>Delete>>Accounts**.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/c20fff6d8d88dd35b95ae7d574abb477.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity** – Select a legal entity from the drop-down to filter accounts.
2.  **Show inactive accounts** – Toggle on to display deactivated accounts.
3.  **Account** – Check one or more account boxes.
4.  **Delete selected accounts** – Click to delete selected account(s).

Previously, when deleting accounts, users had to navigate to the ‘Jobs’ page to view the status and details of what was deleted. Now, all activity is recorded directly in the ‘Delete activity logs,’ and users can view the details and linked job from there, reducing clicks needed and friction when investigating changes.

  
Delete Accounts in Period
----------------------------

From the **Accounts in Period tab**, accounts with the status not prepared or rejected can be deleted. This action removes imported balances, transactions, and any associated work for the specified account in that period. The account will remain active in all other periods and retain all account settings.   

 **HELPFUL HINT**: This functionality is mainly used when group account configurations have been changes, and this needs to be updated in an active period.

    
To delete accounts within a specific period, select the correct period from the left-side menu and navigate to **Administration>>Delete>>Accounts in Period**.   
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/755132385829a907b3101f8cabb9b86d.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity** – Select a legal entity from the drop-down to filter accounts.
2.  **Account** – Check one or more account boxes.
3.  **Delete selected account** – Click to delete the selected account from the period.

Delete Periods
--------------

From the **Periods tab**, unopened, open, and archived periods can be deleted. For an open period to be available, all accounts within the period must have a status of not prepared or rejected.  
Import a balance file with incorrect balances to revert multiple account statuses simultaneously.     

 **HELPFUL HINT**: This functionality is typically used to update a period with incorrect period end type, which can cause accounts that need to be manually reconciled to be automatically finalized.

    
To delete a period, select the correct period from the period drop-down in the left-side menu and navigate to **Administration>>Delete>>Periods**.  
![image.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/ec5d64a74f3b7933e84b36459e909812.jpg)

1.  **Legal entity** – Select the correct legal entity in the drop-down menu. 
2.  **Period** – Check the box(es) for the period(s) to be deleted
3.  **Delete selected periods** – Click to remove the period(s), along with all uploaded balances and completed work. 

 **HELPFUL HINT**: For a period to be deleted, it must be unopened, opened, or archived. In the first two cases, the accounts within that period must have either the not prepared or rejected status. Archived periods can be deleted regardless of account statuses.
