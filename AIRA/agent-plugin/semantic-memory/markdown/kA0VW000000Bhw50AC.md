Adra - How to Contact Support and Manage Your Support Cases
===========================================================

Details
-------

This article provides customers with essential information on how to contact Adra Support, create and manage support cases, and find additional resources for assistance.

Resolution
----------

**Contacting Adra Support:**

1.  **Adra Success Center:**
    
    *   The Adra Success Center is the best place to create, manage, and review your support cases.
    *   Visit: [https://success.adra.com/s/](https://success.adra.com/s/support-center)
    *   Log in with your credentials to submit a new case, track existing cases, and access helpful resources.
2.  **Telephone Support:**
    
    *   For urgent issues or if you prefer to speak directly with a support representative, please call the appropriate number for your region:
        *   **North America:** +1 800 3177998
        *   **EMEA:** +44 (0)870 6260729
        *   **Sweden:** +46 (0) 8725 9102
        *   **Norway:** +47 213 00000
        *   **Denmark:** +45 57808130
        *   **Asia Pacific**: +855 (0)882 7420
        *   **Australia:** +61 28317 1636

3.  **Email Support**
    *   If the case is stalled, you can open the case and use the **Escalate** option. You can also email **[GlobalSupportManagement@trintech.com](mailto:GlobalSupportManagement@trintech.com)** for escalation assistance.

**Frequently Asked Questions:**

*   **Where can I check the status of my support case?**
    
    *   Log in to the Adra Success Center and navigate to the “My Cases” section to view the status and details of your open and closed cases.
*   **How do I escalate a support case?**
    
    *   Within the Success Center, open your case and use the escalation option or contact your regional support number for urgent escalation needs.
*   **Can I update or add information to an existing case?**
    
    *   Yes, you can add comments, upload attachments, and provide additional details directly within your case in the Success Center.
*   **Where can I find product documentation and guides?**
    
    *   The Success Center provides access to product documentation, user guides, release notes, and knowledge base articles.

**Additional Notes:**

*   Ensure your contact information is up to date in the Success Center for timely communication.
*   For non-urgent inquiries, using the Success Center is recommended for the most efficient support experience.
