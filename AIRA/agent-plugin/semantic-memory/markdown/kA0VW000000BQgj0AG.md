Accessing SOC Reports in the Adra Success Center
================================================

Question
--------

How do I access SOC Reports or Bridge Letters?

Details
-------

SOC Reports and Bridge Letters can be accessed through the Success Center at success.trintech.com by logging in with your Success Center credentials.

Steps
-----

From Success Center, navigate through Resources > [Trust Center](https://success.trintech.com/s/resources/trust-center?tabset-b95f9=2) \> select the 'SSAE 18 SOC 1/SOC 2' tab > select the applicable SOC folder to download documentation.

**HELPFUL HINT**: All available SOC Reports can be found under the 'Trust Center' menu. If a report is not listed, it is not yet available. Please check back later or contact your Customer Success representative for an estimated availability timeline.
