Setup View and Manage Trusted Organizations
===========================================

View an Engagement's Trusted Organizations
------------------------------------------

To view organizations trusted by an engagement:  
  
1\. Navigate to **Setup>>Engagements**.  
2\. Click the **Engagement Name** to view its trusted organizations.  
![Click Engagement Name.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/26be317af1ea1e2ab7aaae4205e83de9.jpg)  
When external users, such as implementation team members, are added, the users' organization is trusted. All onboarded employees of a Trusted Organization are available and searchable when the Engagement Admin uses the Connect External User feature in the engagement.  
  
 

Remove Trusted Organizations
----------------------------

BPO Admins and Engagement Admins can remove the following Trusted Organizations from an engagement: Implementation, Support and Auditor (see image below). Any other type of organization must be removed by a Trintech Global System Admin.

 **HELPFUL HINT**: Removing a trusted organization removes any access to the employees of the organization.  
An engagement admin who is an employee of the organization cannot remove their own access.

  
To remove a Trusted Organization from an engagement:  
  
1\. Click the **delete icon**.  
2\. Click **Confirm**.  
![Delete Trusted Organization.png](https://static.adra.com/adra-assistant/images/9bf583b04c1b5a10f3c0cf62aac82d35.jpg)  
BPO Admins and Engagement Admins cannot remove Financial Owners from the list of Trusted Organizations. For assistance with removing a BPO from the engagement's Trusted Organizations, please reach out to Adra support.
